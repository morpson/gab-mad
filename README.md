# GabMad ⚡ (It's MadGab, but free & modern!)

> Sound out the nonsense phrases as fast as you can. First team to shout the real words wins the point!

A free, real-time party word game built with modern web standards, 3D animations, custom procedural lo-fi audio, and browser speech recognition.

---

## 🎮 Gameplay Features

- **100+ Phonetic Puzzles**: Spanning classic party favorites, pop culture, movies, food & drink, famous places, and idioms.
- **Interactive 3D Card Stage**: Flip animations with realistic depth, tilt, and shake on timeouts.
- **Speech Recognition (AI Listener)**: Tap "Listen" to have the browser listen for spoken answers and automatically flip the card when matched!
- **Phonetic Speech Synthesis (TTS)**: Tap "Read Aloud" to hear the browser speak the gibberish phrase phonetically.
- **Procedural Lo-Fi Music & SFX**: Synthesized Web Audio engine that shifts into high gear during countdowns and smoothly ducks audio during active voice listening.
- **Flexible Team Play**: Supports 2 to 4 teams with custom names and neon themes.
- **Tiered Hints**: Progressive hints (Skeleton letter blanks &rarr; Category clues).
- **Party Keyboard Shortcuts**:
  - `[Space]`: Flip / Next card
  - `[H]`: Show hint
  - `[S]`: Read phonetics aloud (TTS)
  - `[M]`: Listen for spoken answer
  - `[P]`: Pause / resume timer
  - `[1 - 4]`: Award point directly to team 1, 2, 3, or 4
  - `[Esc]`: Close dialogs / modals
