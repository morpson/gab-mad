# GabMad

A free party word game where players sound out nonsense phrases to guess the real phrase.

## How to Play

1. Read the gibberish phrase out loud quickly.
2. The sounds will eventually click into a real phrase.
3. The first team to shout the correct answer scores a point.

## Controls

- **Space**: Flip card / Next card
- **H**: Show hint
- **S**: Read phrase aloud
- **M**: Microphone answer detection
- **P**: Pause / Resume timer
- **1–4**: Award point to Team 1–4

## Game setup

- Pick a card category or leave **Shuffle all cards** selected to mix the full deck.
- Choose an avatar for each team, or keep **No avatar**.
- Team wins and the 25 most recent completed games are saved in this browser's local storage. Records follow the team name and stay local to this device and browser.
