GabMad

(it's MadGab, but free)

🗣️

Step One

Read the nonsense — out loud, fast

A card shows you a phrase that looks like absolute gibberish. Your job? Just read it out loud, fast, over and over.

Like this beauty:

Hiney Duck Hiss ↓ (say it fast…) I need a kiss 💋

Every single party has at least one adult yelling that across the room. It's tradition.

🧠

Step Two

Your brain clicks. Then you shout.

Keep repeating it and the sounds will suddenly snap into a real phrase in your head. The moment it clicks — shout it out before anyone else does!

Spa Get Tee Car Bone Ara ↓ Spaghetti Carbonara 🍝

Yes, someone will yell "Spa Get Tee" for 45 seconds straight. That's part of the fun.

⏱️

Step Three

Beat the clock. Score the point.

Each card runs a countdown. Reveal the answer, then tap your team's +1 Point button to score. Most points when the deck runs out wins!

Stuck? Use Hints, have the browser Read It Aloud, or go full sci-fi and tap Listen — shout the answer and the mic auto-detects it.

Sue Purr Cal Lee Fragile Istick… ↓ good luck lol Supercalifragilisticexpialidocious 🎩
🎉

You're ready!

Now set up your teams and start the madness.

Pick 2–4 teams, name them whatever you want, choose how long each round lasts, and hit Start the Show.

Fair warning: the lo-fi music shifts into party mode when the clock is ticking. It's very hard to stay calm.
