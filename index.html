How to Play GabMad

1

Read It Out Loud

A nonsense phrase appears on the card (e.g. "Easel Aid Ease Man"). Sound it out fast!

2

Listen For The Real Words

When you repeat it quickly, phonetics trigger your brain to hear the real phrase ("He's a lady's man").

3

Shout It First To Score

First team to shout the correct answer before the timer runs out gets a point!

4

AI Listening Mode

Tap Listen and shout the answer — browser speech recognition will automatically detect it and flip the card!
