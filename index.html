Step One

Read the nonsense — out loud, fast

A card shows you a phrase that looks like absolute gibberish. Just read it out loud, fast, over and over.

Hiney Duck Hiss ↓ say it fast… I need a kiss 💋
🧠

Step Two

Your brain clicks. Then you shout.

Keep repeating it and the sounds snap into a real phrase. The moment it clicks — shout it out before anyone else does!

Spa Get Tee Car Bone Ara ↓ Spaghetti Carbonara 🍝
⏱️

Step Three

Beat the clock. Score the point.

Each card runs a countdown. Reveal the answer, then tap your team's +1 Point button. Need help? Use Hints, Read Aloud, or try Mic Mode — shout it and the browser detects it.

Sue Purr Cal Lee Fragile Istick… ↓ good luck lol Supercalifragilistic… 🎩

    How to Play GabMad

    1

    Read The Nonsense

    Say the gibberish phrase out loud, fast, over and over. Your brain will decode it.

    2

    Shout The Answer First

    When you hear the real phrase in your head — scream it before your opponents!

    3

    Reveal the answer, award the point to the winning team, then draw the next card.

    4

    Tap Listen and yell the answer — the browser hears it and flips the card automatically!
