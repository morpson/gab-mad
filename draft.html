GabMad

(it’s MadGab, but free)

The party game where a nonsense phrase is a real phrase in disguise — you just have to say it out loud to hear it.

Step one

You get a card like this

Pooh Teal Is Shush

Say it out loud. Again. Faster. Keep going…

It looks like word salad. It is not word salad. Say it enough times and your brain trips over the real phrase hiding inside.

Step two

Then it clicks

The answer is

Bootylicious

You heard it, didn’t you?

There it is! First team to shout the real phrase scores the point. That’s the whole game — and yes, it gets loud.

Step three

Beat the clock

Every card is on a timer, and the music shifts into a faster gear while the clock runs. Feeling brave? Tap Listen during a card and the game itself hears the answer.

Fair warning: full-grown adults will scream “Hiney Duck Hiss” across your living room. That’s “I need a kiss.” It happens every time.

GabMad

(it’s MadGab, but free)

Read the nonsense phrase out loud, over and over — it’s a real phrase in disguise. First team to shout the answer scores.

How to play

One deck, everybody shouting. That’s the whole sport.

1

A card flips up with a phrase that sounds like gibberish. Everyone reads it out loud, fast.

2

Say it enough times and it clicks into a real phrase. First team to shout it wins the card.

3

Beat the timer, tap your team’s button to score, and run up the board before the deck runs out.

Teams

2 to 4 teams. Rename them if you’re feeling fancy.

Round timer

How long each card stays on the clock.

Lo-fi beats run the whole game and shift into a faster gear while the clock is ticking — the note button up top turns them off. During a card, tap Listen and shout the answer: the game hears it and flips the card for you.
On a keyboard: Space draws & reveals cards, H hint, M listen, 1–4 award a point, P pauses the clock.
